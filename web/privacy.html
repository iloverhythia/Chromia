Chromia Privacy Policy

Effective October 22, 2026. Applies to Chromia on Meta Quest, PC, Mac, Linux, Android, iOS and the web.

Chromia is a rhythm game. This page explains what information the game stores and sends, and why. Chromia has no ads, does not sell your information, and does not track you across other apps or websites. It never asks for your real name, email address, contacts, location, camera or microphone.

Stored only on your device

  • Your settings, maps, favorites, map folders, scores and replays.
  • Play tracking for the Settings Generator (how you hit or miss notes). It never leaves your device, and you can turn it off or delete it in Settings > Generator.
  • Crash logs. They stay on your device unless you choose to copy one and send it to us yourself.

Sent to the game's online server

Chromia's online features use the server at savia.nysr.host. It receives:

  • A random player ID created the first time you start the game (not tied to your name or your Meta account), with the game version and platform. It is sent when the game starts, about once a minute while it runs, and when it updates, so we can count players and versions. When you start a map, the map's name is sent too.
  • Map passes, for experience points and leaderboards: your player ID, the map, speed, misses, note count, hit window, the mods you used, a checksum of the map, and a one-way hashed device ID used to stop cheating (the real device ID is never sent).
  • Replays of passed maps: your cursor movement during the map, your game settings and the same hashed device ID.
  • A ban check of your player ID.
  • Discord, only if you link it: your Discord user ID, username and profile picture, to show your name on leaderboards. You can unlink it in the game at any time. On PC, Chromia can also show what you're playing in your Discord status through the Discord app.
  • Multiplayer lobbies, only if you join one: your player name, the lobby and your scores during the match.

Other services the game contacts

  • Map Browser: map lists and downloads come from rhythia.com.
  • Updates and map lists: version checks and data files come from GitHub (github.com, githubusercontent.com, jsDelivr).

Like any internet connection, these services and our server can see your IP address. Their own privacy policies apply to them.

Why we use it

Only to run the game's features (leaderboards, experience points, replays, multiplayer, Discord profiles), to keep them fair, and to understand how many people play which version. We don't use it for advertising and we don't sell or rent it.

How long it's kept and how to delete it

Server data is kept while it's needed for leaderboards and statistics. To have your online data removed, or to ask what we have about you, open a request at github.com/iloverhythia/Chromia/issues (choose the privacy option or write "privacy request"). We'll help you find your player ID and remove the data linked to it. Uninstalling the game deletes everything stored on your device.

Children

Chromia is meant for teens and adults and isn't directed to children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you think a child has used the online features, contact us and we'll delete the data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
