# Notes for the next production release

Add one line per change, starting with "- " (players read these in the
update log). Publishing to production turns them into the new version's notes
in scripts/Savia.gd and empties this list.

- Settings > UI > Show SP: the SP you'd get if you passed right now, at your accuracy so far and your speed, at the top of the right panel (a dash when the map isn't ranked or the play can't give SP, like with No Fail)
- Chat: @mentions. Type @ and the start of a name to pick someone (Tab or click), and you get a notification when someone mentions you, even in the middle of a map
- Chat: opening the Spectate window puts you straight in the chat box, ready to type
- Chat: the text you type shows again when your message has an emoji in it (only the emoji showed)
- Website Game view: smoother playback and cursor, Record video goes fullscreen so the video is your screen's size, and it never shows a crash notice
