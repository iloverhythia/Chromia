# Notes for the next production release

Add one line per change, starting with "- " (players read these in the
update log). Publishing to production turns them into the new version's notes
in scripts/Savia.gd and empties this list.
- Quick restart: the map comes back in right away instead of fading in from black (the wait before the first note is Settings > Gameplay > Lead-In Time)
- Speed keys in the map list: press Ctrl+S, then A / D (or the arrow keys) to go through the speeds, Esc when done
- Your bests and score history are now kept in Chromia's own folder, so opening Rhythia Nightly no longer deletes them (they're copied over the first time you start this version)
- Spectating maps with lots of notes no longer drops frames when you start watching or their game catches up
- Staff can right-click a name in chat to delete that message for everyone or ban the player from chat
- Menus open faster on every device: the menu opens about twice as fast after a song, the Map Editor and Update Log open almost instantly, and the Settings page no longer waits for its preview window
- VR: Settings > VR > Hands > Hand direction: tilt, turn or roll your hand model so it lines up with your beam (the left hand is mirrored)
- Settings > Transfer: Include maps sends all your maps too (with the code over Wi-Fi or in the file); maps the other device already has are skipped
- VR: putting on a skin no longer leaves a black screen until you restart the game
- Map browser (Content Browser > Maps) is much smoother, especially in VR: covers are loaded in the background at card size and the cards appear a few at a time
- Replays (in the game and the website's Game view) now play with the lead-in the player used, so the cursor no longer drifts off the notes when your Lead-In Time is different
- VR (Quest): Settings > VR > Graphics > Unlock refresh rate: always runs at the highest refresh rate your headset offers (like 120 Hz) and keeps it after the headset sleeps
- VR: menus stay smooth: the animated menu background is drawn at lower cost in the headset, and two things that made the menu stutter every few seconds are gone (also on PC and phones)
- The popup when you start a ranked map now shows the SP a full pass gives instead of XP
- VR: switching settings profiles (or putting on a skin) no longer leaves the headset black until you restart: a profile's window settings (fullscreen, maximise, max FPS mode) aren't applied while VR runs
- Website: passes on unranked maps now show in your profile's Recent list with their replay, marked Unranked (no SP or XP)
- Map list: a BOTTOM button under DOWN jumps to the last map, like TOP does for the first
- VR: a snow leopard paw hand, only for breck (player #3)
- Settings > Fonts: 40 more fonts for the song HUD, grouped by look (clean, tall and narrow, sci-fi, pixel and monospace, fun), e.g. Poppins, Montserrat, Bebas Neue, Orbitron, Silkscreen, VT323, Bungee and Comic Neue
- Background Editor > HUD panels: 21 more panel styles, e.g. Neon glow, Pill, Double border, Gradient, Sunset, Ocean, Aurora, Rainbow border, Hologram, Corner brackets, Stripes, Dots, CRT scanlines and Carbon fibre (some of them move)
- Content Browser: maps from Chromia players show their cover picture.
- Content Browser: new "GitHub" choice next to rhythia.com and Chromia players. Paste a link to any GitHub repository (or a folder, release or map file in one) and every .sspm and .rhm map there is listed to download, one by one or all at once.
- The Profile, Leaderboard and Stats tabs on the Profile page have a new look that matches the Online page: bigger tabs, stat cards, tidy numbered rows, and your own row lit up on the leaderboard.
- Spectate: each player's card shows their speed, how far into the map they are and the time they have left, with a progress bar
- Profile page: both panels (Your Profile, and Profile / Leaderboard / Stats) are much less see-through, as solid as the Online page, so they're easy to read over any background.
- Staff: the in-game admin tools now come from the permissions the owner gave your linked Discord account (no admin key any more)
- Maps no longer show RP unless you turn it on: Settings > Replays & Misc > Experimental > Show RP on Maps (off by default). You still earn RP from passes, and your RP total and top 10 RP plays are still in the Profile tab.
- Profile > Leaderboard shows what the website's leaderboard does: each player's country flag, profile picture, tag (Developer, Owner...) and Ranked Team, their level and ranked plays, and how far ahead they are of the next player. Rows are roomier, and clicking one opens that player's profile on the website.
- Maps show their RP by themselves while the Chromia servers can't be reached (after about 12 seconds without a connection), even with Show RP on Maps off, and hide it again once the servers are back.
- Profile, Leaderboard, Stats and Online pages polished: nothing is cut off in the middle of a letter any more (long names end in "…", and leaderboard rows move "Ranked Team" down a line when there's no room), panel corners are smooth, lists don't run under their scrollbar, and the leaderboard, top RP plays and stats tiles have more room.
- Online: hosting a lobby at the default window size no longer squeezes the map's name into one letter per line (the Pick map / Use selected buttons sit under the name now), and the lobby chat's emoji button is small so its hint text fits.
- Switching to another window during a map now gives the mouse back to Windows, and coming back takes it again: fixes the cursor going missing with remote desktop or other games in the background
- Results: the camera button next to the gear (Save video) makes a video of your play (a whole map or the part you played) with your settings: notes, cursor, camera, HUD and background. Its page on the website lets you pick the website's viewer or the game itself (the Game view) to make it, and both make it one frame at a time, so it's a smooth 60 fps however busy the computer is
- Spectating with their settings now shows their own HUD panel picture too
- VR (Quest): a fix for the game closing right after you die with restart on death turned on. The song is now stopped for a moment before it reloads (about a fifth of a second), instead of being swapped out in the same instant.
- The ban screen is no longer a flat picture: it's drawn live in the same look (a slow red glow behind the card, a pulsing warning sign), shows the reason the admin wrote (and its change, within seconds), and plays a clip on a loop under the message (click the clip to mute it).
- Maps with huge numbers of notes (tens of thousands up to a million) load much faster (a 1,000,000-note map: about 7 seconds instead of 36) and no longer freeze or crash the game while they play. The cursor and how notes are judged are untouched: the same hits, misses and score as before.
- Maps whose song is an .m4a (also FLAC, AAC and Opus) play their music: they said the audio was broken. The first time, the song is converted by the Chromia server on the loading screen (a few seconds, needs a connection once); after that it plays from your game's cache, offline too. Add Song takes these files as well.
- Settings has a new look (on by default): the same dark style as the Online and Profile pages on the whole screen, the categories in a list on the left, each group of settings in its own card, and a search box at the top right (or Ctrl+F) that finds a setting in any category: click a result to jump to it. Turn it off in Settings > Replays & Misc > Experimental > New Settings UI for the old settings page.
- Phones (iPhone, iPad, Android): the cursor follows your finger more smoothly and with less delay. No Touch Delay is now on from the start (turn it off in Settings if you prefer), and its prediction was rebuilt: the cursor no longer hops back for a frame in the middle of a swipe (worst at 120 Hz), keeps gliding at high speed instead of moving in steps, works when two touches arrive in one frame (it switched itself off on many Android phones), eases onto your finger when you stop instead of jumping, and also works with Absolute Mode off.
- Cursor trail (the normal one, Smart Trail off): its pieces are spread evenly along the path at any frame rate. At 60 FPS (phones) they bunched up in clumps with gaps between.
- Phones: the file browser has a Sort button (top right): Name, Largest first, Smallest first, Newest first or Oldest first. Folders stay on top, the date shows next to the size when sorting by date, and your choice is kept for next time.
- Settings > Colorsets: the colorset you have selected is marked with an outline and a tick, in your menu's accent colour.
- The new Settings, the Online page, the Profile page with its Profile / Leaderboard / Stats panel, and their windows (Cards, Frames, invites, the lobby map picker) take the colours of your Menu Customizer look: your button colour for filled buttons, tabs and outlines, your accent for headings and values, your text colour, and the dark panels in your button colour's hue. Settings changes as you pick colours; Online and Profile load the menu again once when you next open them. Nothing changes if you have no look of your own.
- New Settings: it no longer shows the old look for a moment when you open it or switch category.
- New Settings > Fonts: every font is shown in its own typeface again (they all looked the same).
- Spectating with someone's settings (and their Save video links) now shows their own custom colorset (it fell back to the default) and plays their own hit and miss sounds
- Replays of VR plays are watched from the player's eyes: their head is the camera and their hands and beam show (in the game, and in Save video's Game view)
- Drag a replay (.sspre) into the game, watch it, then press the camera button on the results to make it into a video on the website
- Settings > Colorsets: the tiles no longer have a shiny chrome frame (the new Settings look was adding it by mistake), and the shine on the selected one is calmer.
- The new Settings, Profile, Leaderboard, Stats and Online panels are slightly see-through again, so a little of the menu background shows.
- Spectating: switching to watch someone else while you're already watching a song no longer plays them at your own speed (e.g. their 1.45x shown at 1x, the cursor off the notes), most often seen in VR
- Replays line the cursor up with the notes more reliably: the lead-in is worked out from the hits themselves (exact, not to the nearest 100 ms), and used over the saved one when that is clearly off
- Cursor trail: much lighter, mostly for phones. The trail's pieces now fade, shrink and take their gradient colour on the graphics card instead of being updated one by one every frame, for both the normal trail and Smart Trail. Having the trail on no longer drags the frame rate down or makes the cursor feel off (long or detailed trails gain the most). It looks the same.
- Phones: Settings has an Old Cursor Movement option (under No Touch Delay and 120 Hz). Turn it on if you preferred how the cursor moved before the smoother prediction; it takes effect in the next song.
- Save video's Game view (and the website's Game view) now loads the player's own note mesh, hit sounds and HUD panel picture: their downloads never finished in the browser version, so it fell back to the default rounded notes
- Spectating someone and their Save video links show their touch indicator style (the rings under their fingers in replays)
- Save video: no more stuttering sound at the very end of videos of a fail or give-up, and hit sounds are no longer doubled (they were louder than in the game)
- Test builds: Settings > Online > Send Passes to the Main Server makes your passes count on the real leaderboards (off by default: they go to the test server)
- Your bests on each map (including the maps you've only failed) can no longer be wiped: they're saved safely (a crash while saving used to leave the file empty, and the next attempt was saved over everything), the previous save is kept as a backup, and a file that can't be read is put aside instead of written over
