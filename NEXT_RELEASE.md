# Notes for the next production release

Add one line per change, starting with "- " (players read these in the
update log). Publishing to production turns them into the new version's notes
in scripts/Savia.gd and empties this list.
- Watching a replay: the replay bar has a Save video button, to make a video of the replay you're watching (it opens your browser, like Save video after a play)
- New mod, 360 Shuffle: like 360, but the spin keeps changing speed and direction. It isn't random: every map has its own pattern, the same every time you play it (and in its replays), so you can learn it. 360 speed sets how fast. It has its own best scores, apart from plain 360
- Blocking without the website: Online > Friends > Blocked has a "Block someone by player number" box, a friend request has a Block button, and the ... menu on a player in Spectate has Block / Unblock (and Add friend). Right-clicking a name in chat still works too
- Settings: right-click a category in the list on the left to hide it ("Hidden" at the bottom of the list shows them again; search still finds what's in them). And a new setting, Replays & Misc > Experimental > Collapsible Groups: click a group's title (Modifiers, Camera...) to fold it shut; it stays that way
- New mod, Blox Saber: no pause cooldown, so you can pause again right after unpausing, and from the very start of a map; unpausing takes a quarter second instead of 0.75. Scores with it aren't submitted (no XP, SP, RP or leaderboard), and it has its own best scores
- Picking a map with an animated (GIF) cover no longer holds the menu up for seconds: only the cover's first picture is read for the still one, not the whole animation.
- When the game freezes (the window stops answering), the next crash report now says what it was stuck on: the step it was in the middle of, the background jobs running, and whether the sound system or the graphics driver had stopped answering. Before, a freeze left nothing to go on.
- The game can no longer wait forever for a map's notes when another job that was reading them stopped half-way.
- The Spectate eye button can be dragged anywhere on the screen (mouse, finger or VR pointer) and stays where you leave it. A plain click still opens Spectate; right-click the eye to put it back in its corner.
- Direct messages: friends can message each other in the game. The chat (the eye button) now has three tabs: Global, DMs and Announcements. DMs lists your friends and conversations with unread counts; messages sent while someone is offline are waiting for them, and a new one shows by the eye button.
- Add a friend without leaving the chat: type a player's name or #number in the DMs tab, and accept or decline friend requests right there. The Friends window (Online > Friends) finds players by name too and has a Message button.
- Announcements tab: the team's latest announcements stay there to read, also the ones sent while you were away or in a map.
- New setting: Auto Skip (Settings > Gameplay, with Restart on Death). The parts of a map that can be skipped (the wait before the first note, long breaks) are skipped by themselves a moment after the "to skip" timer shows. Off by default; not in a lobby, where the host's skip moves everyone.
- Replays: the Note Hitboxes option now shows a white square the size of the hitbox coming in with each note, in front of it, until the note is hit or gone (it was a pile of green and red squares lying on the grid).
- Meta Quest: Passthrough (Settings > VR > Graphics). Play in your real room: the headset's cameras are the background in the menu and in songs, and the menu screen, the grid, notes, cursor, HUD and your hands are drawn over it as solid as ever. The game's own backgrounds aren't drawn while it's on. It needs the app's newest version installed from its file (the Quest app has to ask the headset for passthrough, which an in-app update can't add): until then the option is greyed out.
- Defeats on Discord: beating a hard ranked map is posted in the Discord's defeats channel (the very first to beat one is marked First victor), and so is a new best % when you fail on one, if it's at or above that map's list %. Only at 1x speed or faster, and with the same rules as a ranked pass
- Lobbies: everyone plays with the host's lead-in time (it was always 3 seconds). Only for that game: your own lead-in setting isn't changed and is back once you're in the menu
- Fixed the game closing by itself right as the menu opened at startup. It happened when the intro finished (or was skipped) before the game was done loading, most of all while the "You are on an outdated version" box was waiting for an answer. The start screen now waits for loading to finish
- Fixed the game closing when someone watching you didn't have the map and it was very big (over 60 MB): it was read whole into memory to send to the server, which only takes maps up to 60 MB anyway. Now it isn't read, and the watcher is told the map is too big. Publishing a map over 60 MB says so instead of trying
- The loading screen before a map (in a lobby too) no longer just sits on black when something holds it up: after a few seconds it says what it's waiting for, and Esc goes back to the menu. Waiting for a map's notes that another job was reading used to stop the whole game (a black, frozen screen); it now keeps going and takes over after 15 seconds
- The 360 mod's speed is only shown when it isn't normal ("360 Shuffle" instead of "360 Shuffle x1", e.g. on your Discord status)
- Less lag for players with many prestige (or other moving) frames and cards: each frame is about three times quicker to draw, and frames and cards scrolled out of sight in a list are no longer drawn. Moving cards are drawn 30 times a second instead of every frame (15 on Quest, in VR and on phones)
- Replays: a slider next to Cursor Path sets how long the path is (0.1 to 5 seconds behind the cursor). The website's Game view now has the replay bar too: scrub, change the speed, turn on the drawings, and Hide it (the small logo brings it back). Not while it's recording a video
- No Delay (Settings > Video & Audio, Experimental): no more stutters in busy parts of a map where the picture stood still for a tenth of a second or more while the game ran on. With no frame limit at all the game made over a thousand frames a second, the graphics card was full and Windows (and anything recording) couldn't get the frames to the screen in time. No Delay now holds the frames to four times your screen's refresh rate (240 to 1,000 a second, or your Max FPS if lower); every frame still uses your newest mouse movement
- New map file: Chromia maps (.chrm), with things Sound Space maps can't have. First: lyrics, made the way Novastra does them. In the Map Editor open Edit Extra Objects, set GAME to Chromia (or Novastra) and pick Lyrics: type a lyric and press Enter (or CREATE) to place it at the current time, with Fade In / Fade Out if you like. Lyrics add on to the line shown; "-" in front starts a new line, "-" at the end joins the next lyric on as a syllable, and an empty lyric clears the line. In songs the lyrics show inside the grid near its bottom edge. Export > Chromia (CHRM) saves a .chrm; Import Map, dropping a .chrm on the game and the editor's Load Map all open them. Maps saved from the editor into your map list keep their lyrics too, and so does a map sent to a lobby
- Settings (old look, and Sound Space Mode): right-click a tab to hide it (the same menu shows hidden ones again), and Collapsible Groups gives each group a title you click to fold it
- Pressing Enter in the 360 speed box now leaves the box
- Speed keys (Ctrl+S in the map list): Space or the retry key no longer types into the map search, even when you were typing in it before
- Browser version: maps from hosts that don't allow browser downloads (most Rhythia maps) now download, through the Chromia server, instead of saying "This map's host doesn't allow downloads from the browser"
- Being spectated no longer makes your game stutter when someone starts watching mid-song: your look is sent without reading your background, mesh and sound files again, and a map sent to a spectator who doesn't have it is read a little at a time
- Speed keys (Ctrl+S): from a custom speed, A / D go to the nearest speed that way (instead of back to Normal), and D past ++++ goes to your custom speed
- Save video (Game view): the song is no longer nearly silent after a pause in the play (it kept the volume it had the moment the music came back in)
- After unpausing, the music fades back in to your Music Volume instead of full volume (and in replays it no longer keeps getting louder afterwards)
- Watching someone's replay from the leaderboard plays their own hit and miss sounds (the ones they play with), and yours again after
- Meta Quest: a new install starts with the fastest graphics in songs (the same as Settings > VR > "Best frame rate": foveated rendering High, the plain background in songs, glow off), so the headset holds its 72 Hz from the first song. Foveated rendering on Auto is now High on the Quest (dynamic: it only lowers the edges when a frame needs it). Frame Smoothing's rate drop never goes below 72 Hz in a headset. Your saved settings are kept
- Trophies: a new stat with a little trophy, one for every ranked map where you have the #1 pass. They show on your profile panel, your Profile page, Spectate > View stats and next to everyone's name in a multiplayer lobby. The leaderboard (Profile > Leaderboard) has a Trophies tab next to Additive, and a Top Passes tab too (everyone's best pass on each ranked map, by SP: the map, accuracy, speed and misses)
- Time played (Profile, Stats) no longer counts the empty parts of a map you skip (your own skips and a lobby host's), and neither does the level experience that goes with it
- Casino in the game: type /casino in a chat to open the casino window, with the same ten games, chips, daily bonus, leaderboard and Give chips as the Discord server's /casino. Your chips are your linked Discord account's, so they're the same on Discord and in the game (link Discord on your Profile first). A game left open in one place can be finished in the other
- D. Jarvis, the official Chromia bot, is in the game: always online (at the bottom of the Online list, with a BOT tag), with the Discord bot's picture and "Official Chromia Bot" on his profile. Say "@D. Jarvis" in the global chat, or add him as a friend (he says yes at once) and message him, and he answers the way the Discord Jarvis does. Admins can play as him: Profile > Play as D. Jarvis (and Back to my account)
- Chat commands: /casino opens the casino; staff have /mute <name or #number> <how long> [reason] (30m, 3d, 2 centuries, 1 eon, forever...: it always says how long), /unmute <name or #number> and /adminpanel (opens the admin panel). A very long chat mute shows how long in words ("2 centuries left")
- The 360 Shuffle mod has its own icon (crossed arrows) next to the 360 one while you play
- Map Editor: CREATE NEW MAP asks for a Sound Space map or a Chromia map (the classic editor look has Chromia with the other games). A Chromia map (also one opened from a .chrm or one with lyrics) has its own tools in the main panels: GRAPHICS > Lyrics (CHROMIA) to type a lyric and place it where the song is (Enter or CREATE, Fade In / Fade Out), and ON TIMELINE to move or change them on the track. Lyrics show as small marks over the track and the line shows above the timeline. It exports as a .chrm
