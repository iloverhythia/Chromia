# Notes for the next production release

Add one line per change, starting with "- " (players read these in the
update log). Publishing to production turns them into the new version's notes
in scripts/Savia.gd and empties this list.
- Score history (map screen > History) is bigger and easier to read, and shows Discord profile pictures instead of a letter (yours, and whoever played on this device)
- NEW: click an attempt in your score history to watch its replay (works for older attempts too, when Record Replays was on)
- Spectating with their settings: you see their cursor picture too (it goes to the server with your settings when you play)
